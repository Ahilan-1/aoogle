Introducing banner ads

arlong is introducing a simple, honest advertising model. A single banner ad appears on the homepage portal — never in search results, never following users around the web. There are no trackers, no pixels, no cookies, and no profiling. Your ad is a fixed image displayed to everyone who visits arlong.com during your rental period.

Ads only appear on the homepage. Search results remain completely ad-free. We will never insert promoted results, sponsored listings, or tracking scripts into search pages — ever.

Meet "Renting" — our ad model

You don't buy impressions or clicks. You rent the ad space for a fixed duration. During that time, your banner shows on the homepage for every visitor. Simple, transparent, and predictable.

How it works

Getting your ad on arlong takes just a few steps:

  1. 1 Log in to your arlong account. Don't have one? Community accounts are free and require no personal information.
  2. 2 Go to the Ad Maker panel at /admaker. This is where you create and manage your ad campaigns.
  3. 3 Submit your banner image URL. Your banner must be hosted somewhere online (see tips below). Recommended dimensions: 560 × 110 px. Supported formats: PNG, JPEG, GIF.
  4. 4 Provide contact details — your email address and Telegram handle. These are used only to communicate about your ad campaign.
  5. 5 Choose your duration using the calculator above. Pick anything from 6 hours up to 240 hours.
  6. 6 Submit for admin review. We manually review each ad to ensure it meets our quality guidelines. This usually takes less than 24 hours.
  7. 7 Make payment to ahilan290@gmail.com via UPI or PayPal once your ad is approved. The amount is the total from the calculator based on your chosen duration.
  8. 8 Send payment proof by replying to the approval email or forwarding the transaction receipt to the same address. Your ad goes live within minutes of confirmation.

Getting your banner image URL

To submit an ad, you need your banner image hosted online so we can display it. Here are some free options:

  • Imgur — Upload at imgur.com/upload, then right-click the image and select "Copy image address" to get the direct URL ending in .png or .jpg.
  • Postimages — Go to postimages.org, upload your banner, and copy the "Direct link" provided.
  • ImgBB — Upload at imgbb.com, then open the image and copy the direct link from the "BBCode full" or "Direct link" option.
  • Your own server — If you have a website, host the image there and provide the full URL (e.g., https://yoursite.com/banner.png).

No tracking, no targeting

We mean it. arlong's advertising model is built on the same privacy-first principles as the rest of the search engine:

  • No user tracking. We don't use cookies, pixels, browser fingerprints, or any other tracking technology. Your ad is shown to everyone equally.
  • No search result ads. Ads never appear in search results. The homepage is the only ad placement, and it's clearly marked.
  • No targeting. We don't segment users by demographics, interests, location, or browsing history. Your ad reaches the entire arlong audience.
  • No retargeting. We don't follow users who see your ad. There is no retargeting pixel, no ad network integration, no third-party scripts.

Privacy-first means exactly that. Your ad gets visibility; users get privacy. Everyone wins.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run multiple ads at the same time?
Only one banner ad is shown on the homepage at any given time. Ads are scheduled back-to-back. You can purchase multiple consecutive rental periods to extend your total run time.
What happens if my ad is rejected?
We'll tell you exactly why and suggest changes. Common reasons: incorrect dimensions, inappropriate content, or a broken image URL. You can resubmit a revised ad at no extra cost.
Can I cancel or change my ad after it goes live?
Once your ad is live, it runs for the full rental period. Cancellations or mid-run changes are not supported. Make sure your banner and duration are final before submitting payment.
What content is allowed?
Ads must comply with our content guidelines: no adult content, gambling, crypto schemes, political campaigning, or misleading claims. If you're unsure, email us first at ahilan290@gmail.com.
How do I know my ad is actually showing?
You can visit arlong.com during your rental period to see your banner live. We also send a confirmation email when the ad goes live and another when the rental period ends.
Do you offer discounts for longer durations?
The rate is a flat $0.30/hour regardless of duration. However, longer rentals give you more total visibility since your ad stays up for more hours. The maximum rental of 240 hours (10 days) costs $72.00.
Can I pay with cryptocurrency?
At the moment we accept payments via UPI and PayPal only. Payments are sent to ahilan290@gmail.com. If you need an alternative payment method, reach out to us directly.
