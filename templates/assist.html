Search Assist

The web,
distilled.

Search Assist turns any query into a concise, cited overview — generated only when you ask, kept private by default, and always under your control.

01

Summaries, on request

Nothing is generated unless you ask. The page behind a search is yours to explore without a layer of interpretation between you and the source.

02

Quotes every claim

Every summary line carries its source, so you can trace any statement back to the page it came from — and judge it for yourself.

03

Private by default

No profiles, no ad tracking, no fingerprints. Search history stays in your control and AI features are strictly opt-in.

04

You stay in charge

Regional and language preferences tune results to you. Adjust them any time in settings, or read the full privacy policy.
