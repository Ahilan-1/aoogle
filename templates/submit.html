How to submit

  1. Prepare your site — Make sure your content is original, substantive, and well-written. Remove auto-playing media, pop-ups, and aggressive ads.
  2. Send your submission — Email or DM us your domain, a 2–3 sentence description, and why you think your site belongs in a quality-focused index.
  3. Review period — We manually review every submission within 7 business days. We audit 3–5 pages for quality, originality, and value.
  4. Decision — If accepted, your domain enters our crawl list. If rejected, we explain why and you may resubmit after addressing the issues.

Frequently asked

How long does it take?

We review submissions within 7 business days. Complex sites may take longer.

Can I resubmit if rejected?

Yes. Address the issues mentioned in your rejection reason and resubmit. Repeated low-effort submissions will be ignored.

Do you accept paid submissions?

No. We never accept payment for inclusion. Any site that reaches out offering payment will be permanently blacklisted.

My site is already in your index. How do I update it?

Our crawler refreshes periodically. If you've made significant improvements, email us and we'll prioritize a re-review.
