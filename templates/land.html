FAQ

Questions?

How is arlong different from Google?+
arlong is an independent search engine that doesn't track you, profile you, or sell your data. We don't run ads in search results, and we don't build a profile of who you are. Google makes money by collecting your data — we make money through optional premium subscriptions only.
Is my search history saved?+
No. We don't log or store your search history. Each search is anonymous and independent. If you create an account, we only store your preferences — not what you searched for.
How do you make money?+
Through optional Premium plans ($4/month) that give you more searches per day, AI summaries, and priority support. That's it. No ads, no data selling, no investor pressure to grow at any cost.
What is the AI summary feature?+
We offer optional AI summaries powered by Groq to give you quick answers. But we believe AI should be your choice — not forced on you. If you like AI for productivity, you can enable it anytime. If you prefer a pure, AI-free search experience, just disable it in settings. Your search, your rules.
Can I remove my site from the index?+
Yes. We respect robots.txt and offer a blocklist system. Site owners can request removal or blocking through our policy page.
