So what is this?

arlong is a search engine I'm building by hand. It pulls results from multiple sources (DDG, Brave, Google, and more) and gives you clean, ad-free results.

There's no venture capital, no growth-at-all-costs mindset, no "let's harvest user data and figure out monetization later." It's just me, building something I actually want to use.

It's still growing. The index is small compared to Google, but for most everyday searches, it works pretty well. Try it and see.

What you get

🔒

No tracking. At all.

I don't log your searches, build profiles, or sell anything to advertisers. Your searches stay between you and your screen.

🔐

Encrypted queries

Your search is encrypted in your browser before it ever leaves your device. No plaintext queries in URLs, logs, or browser history. AES-256-GCM encryption, end-to-end.

🚫

Zero ads in results

Every result you see is organic. No sponsored links sneaking in at the top, no "promoted" results pretending to be real answers.

🤖

AI is optional, not forced

There's an AI summary feature if you want it. If you don't, turn it off in settings and you'll never see it. Your choice.

🌍

Built different

Bangs for 75+ sites, image search, video search, dark mode, community blogs, and a free search API. More coming soon.

Questions people actually ask

Is this just another privacy pitch?+
Partly, yeah. But it's also just a better search experience. No ads cluttering your results, no "People also ask" spam, no SEO-farmed listicles at the top. You search, you get real results, you move on with your day.
Do you really not save my searches?+
Nope. There's no search history tied to your session. If you make an account, we save your preferences (like theme, AI toggle) but not what you searched for. Ever.
How do you keep the servers running?+
There's an optional Premium plan ($4/month) that gives you more daily searches and unlocks AI summaries. Most people won't need it — free tier is pretty generous. No ads, no data selling, no investor pressure.
What's with the AI summaries?+
It uses Groq to generate quick summaries of search results. Handy for stuff like "what is X" or quick comparisons. But it's off by default — you turn it on in settings if you want it. Some people hate AI, some people find it useful. I think you should choose.
Can I get my site removed?+
Sure. We respect robots.txt. If your site shouldn't be indexed, let us know through the policy page and we'll handle it.
