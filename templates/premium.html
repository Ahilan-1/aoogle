Pricing questions

How do credits work?

Every successful operation deducts its published credit cost from one shared balance. Included credits are used before prepaid credits.

Do unused credits expire?

Free includes 100 one-time credits with no expiry or refresh. Paid-plan included credits reset on the plan schedule and do not roll over. Purchased prepaid credits do not expire.

Is the annual saving real?

Yes. Twelve $5 Pro months total $60, while Pro Annual is displayed at $52 per year—a genuine $8 saving.

Will checkout be in USD?

USD is the public display currency. Dodo may present a supported local currency and applicable taxes before you approve payment.
