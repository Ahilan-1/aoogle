Encrypted Search

All search queries on arlong are encrypted client-side using AES-256-GCM before being sent to our servers. This means:

  • Your search query is encrypted in your browser before it ever leaves your device.
  • The server never sees your plaintext query in the URL or server logs.
  • Your search query does not appear in browser history, bookmarks, or URL bars.
  • When you click a search result, the destination website does not receive your search query in the referrer header.

This encryption is applied on top of HTTPS transport encryption, providing defense-in-depth protection for your search privacy. Your queries remain encrypted end-to-end between your browser and our search engine.
