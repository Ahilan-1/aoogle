Why Arlong
For humans. For agents.
Zero bloat either way.
Arlong is a search layer that sits between you and the raw web. It filters, evaluates, compresses, and cites — so you only see what matters, whether you're a person or a pipeline.
[S]

Neural Injection Shield

Every page is screened for prompt injection before it enters any context. Malicious payloads are hard-blocked. Suspicious signals are flagged for review. Your AI never sees them.

Active
[A]

Agentic Multi-Hop Search

A search planner decomposes your question, runs parallel sub-queries, grounds each answer in full-page fetches, then re-answers until the result is complete. Multi-step reasoning with completeness checks.

Multi-hop
[E]

AI Evaluation Per Link

Before you click anything, Arlong scores it. Relevance, trust, timeliness — every result gets a structured evaluation so you know exactly what to expect.

Scored
